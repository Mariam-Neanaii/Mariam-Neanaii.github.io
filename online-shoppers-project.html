Project Overview

This project analyzes online shoppers' behavior to understand the factors that influence whether a visitor makes a purchase.

The analysis focuses on identifying meaningful patterns in customer sessions and transforming those findings into insights that could help improve e-commerce conversion rates.

Business Problem

E-commerce businesses receive many website visitors, but only a percentage of those visitors complete a purchase.

The objective of this analysis was to explore customer behavior and identify patterns associated with purchasing intentions.

Key Questions

  • Which customer behaviors are associated with a higher likelihood of making a purchase?
  • How do different types of website visits affect purchasing intentions?
  • Are there patterns related to returning versus new visitors?
  • How do factors such as page visits, time spent, and visitor characteristics relate to conversion?
  • What insights could help improve e-commerce conversion rates?

Analysis Process

1. Understanding the Dataset

Examined the available variables to understand website visitor behavior, session characteristics, page interactions, and purchasing outcomes.

2. Data Cleaning

Checked the dataset for missing values, inconsistent data, and variables requiring preparation before analysis.

3. Exploratory Data Analysis

Explored distributions and relationships between visitor characteristics, browsing behavior, and purchasing intentions.

4. Customer Behavior Analysis

Compared different visitor groups and investigated behavioral patterns that may be associated with conversion.

5. Data Visualization

Created visualizations to clearly communicate important patterns, trends, and differences in customer behavior.

6. Business Insights

Translated the analytical findings into practical insights relevant to improving customer experience and conversion rates.
